# Aramesh Music Academy

An informational website for a Persian-language music academy. Its purpose is to introduce the academy and its capabilities to visitors — not to handle enrollment, payment, or student accounts.

## Language

**Visitor**:
A person browsing the site to learn about the academy. Primary audience is parents researching lessons for their children; secondary audience is adult learners.
_Avoid_: User, customer, client

**Academy**:
Aramesh Music Academy itself — the business this site represents.
_Avoid_: School, institute

**ClassSession**:
A recurring time slot when a Teacher is present at the academy on a given weekday. A single ClassSession covers one or more Instruments taught by that teacher during that slot — because a teacher may teach multiple instruments to different students simultaneously. The schedule table groups rows by Teacher, with instruments displayed as a list within each row.
_Avoid_: Slot, block, period, course

**Testimonial**:
A short quote from a satisfied parent displayed on the home page, between the teacher preview and the contact call-to-action. Its purpose is social proof — to tip an undecided parent toward making contact.
_Avoid_: Review, comment, feedback

**Video**:
A concert or performance video embedded from YouTube or Aparat (not uploaded directly). Lives inside the gallery structure under a dedicated top-level category.
_Avoid_: Upload, hosted video

**GalleryPage**:
A two-level category structure for photos. Top-level pages are broad categories (e.g. "کنسرت‌ها"); their children are specific events or albums (e.g. "کنسرت بهار ۱۴۰۳"). Nesting beyond two levels is not used and should be prevented in the admin.
_Avoid_: Album, folder, directory

**Conversion**:
The desired outcome of a visit: the visitor contacts the academy (form, phone, or in-person visit) and ultimately enrolls. The site exists to move visitors toward this. Online enrollment, student login, payment, and student portals are explicitly out of scope.
_Avoid_: Sign-up, registration
